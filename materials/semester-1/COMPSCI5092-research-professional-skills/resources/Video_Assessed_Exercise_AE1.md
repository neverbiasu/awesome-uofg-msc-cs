# Video_Assessed_Exercise_AE1

The Professional Skills Video Assessed Exercise (20%)  Team Allocation:  For this assessment, you have been randomly allocated into teams of 5 students.  Please see the Moodle (e.g. the Participants tab of the Moodle) for your group allocation.  If you are not allocated a group or if you have a good cause for switching groups please let  me know as soon as possible and we can sort it out.  Task Overview:  The task of this exercise is for your team to create a video of maximum length 90 seconds.  In this context a “video” means a stream of visual information that is presented over time,  with or without an accompanying audio stream. There are no additional requirements  specified   -   it is for you to decide what you will put in your video and how you will create it.  You are (deliberately) being given freedom to make your own decisions about the nature of  your video.  Specific Task Information:  The “Code of Conduct for British Computer Society (BCS) Members” has four sections  (numbered 1 - 4), within which there are sub - sections (referred to by letters between a and g).  Choose   one sub - section   of this Code of Conduct that interests you.  1 -   In your video, present a realistic scenario that relates to a breach of the Code under that  sub - section.  The scenario does not have to be real (that is, it does not have to be an event  that has really happened), but it must be realistic (that is, even if it comes  from your imagination, it must be possible that it might happen).  2 -   As part of your scenario presentation you must consider the stakeholders and the  effect of the breach on them.  3 -   You must also consider the possible outcomes of the breach of the code, and their  likelihood.  The Use of GenAI to generate the video/ images/ or voice - over is not allowed in this AE
Submission Information:  Only the team leader should submit this assignment. Other members of the team should not  submit anything at all.  The Moodle submission (made by the team leader) must consist of two things:  ● A link to a YouTube video.  ● A text file listing the names and student numbers of all the students in the team and  the Number, Letter and full text of the BCS Code of Conduct sub - section that you  have chosen. It should also describe any external support or resources you have  used in the preparation of your video.  The first screen of the video must list the names and student numbers of all the students in the  team, and must clearly indicate the Number, Letter and full text of the BCS Code of Conduct  subsection that you have chosen.  Your work will be assessed with respect to the following criteria:  ● How realistic is the scenario?  ● How well does it demonstrate the purpose of the chosen sub - section of the code?  ● How well have stakeholders and outcomes been considered?  Generic feedback on the quality of the submissions will be given to the whole class.  Miscellaneous Information:  Videos that are significantly longer or shorter than 90 seconds will incur a two - band penalty.  Submission Date:  Friday 28 November 16:30
