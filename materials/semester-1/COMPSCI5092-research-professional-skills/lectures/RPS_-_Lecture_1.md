## Research Methods

### What is research?
“Research is a process of systematic inquiry that entails collection of data; documentation of critical information; and analysis and interpretation of that data/information, in accordance with suitable methodologies set by specific professional fields and academic disciplines.”
(Source: https://www.hampshire.edu/dof/what-is-research)

“Research is what I’m doing when I don’t know what I’m doing.”
(Wernher von Braun)

- The systematic investigation into and study of materials and sources in order to establish facts and reach new conclusions. (OED)
- A search or investigation directed to the discovery of some fact by careful consideration or study of a subject; a course of critical or scientific inquiry. (OED)
- The creation of new knowledge and/or the use of existing knowledge in a new and creative way so as to generate new concepts, methodologies and understandings. (A framework for research and research training, 2001)

For us, research is about solving problems. These might be practical problems or intellectual problems. Research involves discovering or creating a solution to a problem. This involves understanding the problem, and understanding the context of the problem. Then it involves creating a solution, and evaluating that solution. This is very much like design. Research is a form of design.

### Research skills
- Identifying a problem
- Understanding the context of the problem
- Finding out what is already known about the problem
- Coming up with a new solution to the problem
- Evaluating the solution
- Communicating the solution to others

These are the skills we will be teaching in this course, which are essential for your dissertation and a successful career as a computer scientist.

## The Course

This course covers research methods, professional skills, and study skills to prepare you for your dissertation and future career.

- **Teaching:** The course is taught by a team of lecturers, each covering a specific topic block.
- **Assessment:** Assessment is through a portfolio of work submitted at the end of the semester.

### Course Themes
- Research methods
- Professional skills
- Study skills

### Course Structure
The course consists of lectures and tutorials. Tutorials provide an opportunity for discussion, questions, and feedback on your work.

### Assessment Portfolio
The portfolio is assessed to generate a final mark and includes:
- A literature search
- A critical review of a research paper
- A research proposal
- A short presentation
- A reflective report

The portfolio is submitted electronically, marked by lecturers, moderated by an external examiner, and approved by the board of examiners.
