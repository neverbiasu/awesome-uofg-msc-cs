__Issues raised in EvaSys questionnaires__

Time & Scheduling Concerns

We are aware that having two separate hourly slots is not ideal for this course, but due to timetabling constraints this year, it has been difficult to secure a continuous two\-hour block\. Since the course is organized in weekly units, there were some weeks when the material could be covered in a single hour, and so we opted to cancel the second slot in those cases\. It’s also worth clarifying that we have not run any lectures fully online\. We hope to be able to secure a single two\-hour block in future years\.

AY 2025\-26

Iraklis and Nikela

Coursework complexity & Structure

Thank you for sharing your concerns regarding the course’s broad scope and workload\. The goal is to give a well\-rounded experience that reflects real\-world web development\. We will consider adjustments to the schedule of the assessments to help students focus more effectively on the key skills and reduce the overall pressure\.

Content & Updates

Thank you for the feedback on the course content\. While we strive to update the course content annually, we recognize that some components would benefit from an update\. Our plan is to review and reorganize some sections to ensure that the content is better aligned with industry practices and more intuitive to learners\.
