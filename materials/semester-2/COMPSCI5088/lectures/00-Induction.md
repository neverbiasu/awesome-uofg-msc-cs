What is this course 
about
?
The Big Data course is designed to teach you how to 
identify big data problems 
and 
provide you the tools to solve those problems
Broadly it covers three main aspects
Theory
 of what Big Data is and why it’s a problem
Technologies to enable 
processing
 of Big Data
Technologies to enable the 
storage and access 
of Big Data
