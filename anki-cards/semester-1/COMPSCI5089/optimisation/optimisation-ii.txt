What is the intuition behind Momentum optimiser?	It adds a "velocity" component to smooth out updates and overcome bumps
What is the key idea of Nesterov Accelerated Gradient (NAG)?	It anticipates where it's going and corrects its course
What is the main feature of the AdaGrad optimiser?	It adapts the learning rate for each parameter individually
What is a major drawback of AdaGrad?	The learning rate can shrink to zero, stopping learning entirely
How does RMSprop improve upon AdaGrad?	It uses an exponentially decaying average of squared gradients, preventing vanishing learning rates
What two ideas does the Adam optimiser combine?	Momentum and RMSprop
Why is Adam often the default choice in deep learning?	It is robust and effective with little hyperparameter tuning
What kind of information does Newton's Method use?	Second-order information (the curvature of the function) via the Hessian matrix
What is the main disadvantage of Newton's Method for large models?	Computing the inverse of the Hessian matrix is extremely computationally expensive
