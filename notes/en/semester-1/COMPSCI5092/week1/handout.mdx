# Unassessed Exercises / Problems: Week 1 Handout

## Problem 1: Intellectual Property Scenario

Helen produced a series of lecture videos for the Research Professional Skills course in 2020 whilst working at the university. In 2022 Helen left the university and requested her lecture videos not be reused. Joseph, who took over the Research Professional Skill course, argued the videos Helen produced belonged to the university and not Helen. Later on, while teaching the course, Joseph during a lecture used a set of lecture slides Helen had created.

**1a) Why might Joseph make the argument the videos Helen produced belonged to the university and not her? What legal act might he have referred to while making his claim?**

**1b) Did Joseph plagiarise Helen?**

## Problem 2: Prioritizing Legal Issues

Which of the following legal issues affects you most?

A. Intellectual Property Rights
B. Confidential Information
C. Data Protection and Privacy
D. The Computer Misuse Act

Put them in priority order, giving an example for each that relates to your own context.

## Problem 3: Societal Impact of IT

Consider the following aspects of society. Choose 1 where you think that the increase in the use of IT has had a negative impact, and 1 where you think that the increase in the use of IT has had a positive impact.

For each of the 2 aspects you have chosen, find at least 1 published source that supports your view; write a short (max 100 words) paragraph discussing the difference between how things used to be (before the use of IT became prevalent), and how things are now.

**The Societal Aspects:**
- Private Life
- Commerce
- Work
- Education
- Gambling
- Access to IT
- Sport
- Communication
- Digital footprint

---

*[Advice: don't choose the easy options that you already know something about. Rather, choose to explore ones that are new to you – they will be much more interesting!]*
