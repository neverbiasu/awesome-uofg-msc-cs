## Exam Structure

- **Format:** Open book, online assessment.
- **Total Marks:** 80
- **Number of Questions:** 4 (Answer all)

## Question Breakdown

### Question 1: Linear Algebra
- **Topics:** Data whitening and solving for a transformation matrix.
- **Key Concepts:**
    - Describing the full process of whitening a dataset, including matrix dimensions.
    - Explaining the effect of whitening on specific variables and its desirability.
    - Formulating a method to calculate a transformation matrix `M` from point correspondences (P' = MP).
    - Extending the approach to handle more than two point correspondences, leading to a least-squares problem.

### Question 2: Optimisation
- **Topics:** Interpretation of loss landscapes and algorithm trajectories.
- **Key Concepts:**
    - Qualifying points on a contour plot (e.g., minimum, maximum, saddle point) and describing the expected gradient.
    - Identifying optimisation algorithms (Gradient Descent, Hill-Climbing, Newton's Method) based on their plotted trajectories.
    - Discussing potential issues with iterative algorithms (e.g., local minima) and proposing mitigation strategies.
    - Explaining how to use constraints to avoid specific minima.

### Question 3: Probabilities
- **Topics:** Expectation and conditional probability.
- **Key Concepts:**
    - Calculating the expectation for different variables from a dataset.
    - Calculating the expectation of a weighted combination of variables.
    - Estimating conditional probabilities from data (e.g., pass rate for different groups).
    - Describing a high-level approach to tune weights to achieve a target outcome (e.g., a specific pass rate).

### Question 4: Databases
- **Topics:** Storage, I/O costs, and join strategies.
- **Key Concepts:**
    - Calculating the blocking factor and the number of blocks required for relations.
    - Performing a cost analysis of a nested loop join, comparing efficiency based on which relation is chosen as the outer one.
    - Defining "Selection Cardinality" and explaining its importance in query optimisation.
