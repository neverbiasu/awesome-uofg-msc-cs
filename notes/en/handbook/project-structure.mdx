# 📌 Table 1: Programme Structure and Academic Planning
| **🎓 Programme Structure & Planning** | **📌 Key Points at a Glance** |
|--|--|
| **Total Credits** | **180 credits** = 120 taught (S1+S2) + 60 project (S3) |
| **Progression Requirements** | ✅ GPA ≥ 12.0<br/>✅ 120 total credits<br/>✅ ≥90 credits at M-level<br/>✅ ≥90 credits at ≥D3<br/>✅ No G/H grades |
| **Degree Classification** | 🥇 **Distinction**: Overall GPA ≥ 17.5 + Project ≥ B1<br/>🥈 **Merit**: Overall GPA ≥ 14.5 + Project ≥ C1<br/>✅ **Pass**: Project ≥ D3 |
| **Exit Awards** | 🎓 **PGDip**: 120 credits, GPA ≥ 9.0, ≥80 credits at ≥D3<br/>📜 **PGCert**: 60 credits, GPA ≥ 9.0, ≥40 credits at ≥D3 |
| **Semester Schedule** | 🗓️ S1: Sep-Dec<br/>🗓️ S2: Jan-May<br/>🗓️ S3: Jun-Sep (Project period) |

## 🎯 Key Points
- **180 credits** is the total requirement for the full MSc degree.
- A **GPA of 12.0** is the minimum threshold to progress to the project stage.
- **Distinction** requires excellent overall performance, not just a high GPA.
- **Exit awards** provide alternative qualifications for students unable to complete the full programme.

## 📅 Timeline Planning Suggestions
1.  **S1 Phase**: Adapt to the learning pace and ensure foundational courses are passed.
2.  **S2 Phase**: Aim for high scores to boost your overall GPA.
3.  **S3 Phase**: Focus on the project to achieve an excellent grade.
