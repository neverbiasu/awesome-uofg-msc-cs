## 🎯 Elements of a Successful Project

### 📋 Planning Phase (During S2)
- **Topic Selection**: Thoroughly discuss research direction with your supervisor.
- **Literature Review**: Build a solid theoretical foundation.
- **Methodology Design**: Define your research methods and technical approach.

### 🏃‍♂️ Execution Phase (During S3)
- **Regular Reporting**: Meet with your supervisor weekly or bi-weekly.
- **Progress Management**: Stick to the plan and adjust as needed.
- **Documentation**: Continuously document your research process and findings.

### 📝 Completion Phase
- **Dissertation Writing**: Ensure clear structure and logical rigor.
- **Quality Checks**: Multiple rounds of revision and refinement.
- **Timely Submission**: Strictly adhere to the deadline.

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls
- **Remote Work**: Seriously affects visa status; avoid at all costs.
- **Insufficient Communication**: Maintain close contact with your supervisor.
- **Time Management**: 12 weeks is a tight schedule; use your time efficiently.
- **Scope Control**: Avoid a project scope that is too large to complete.

## 🆘 Support Resources
- **Supervisor Guidance**: For academic and technical support.
- **Peers**: For mutual exchange and support.
- **University Facilities**: Labs, library, and other resources.
- **IT Support**: For solutions to technical problems.
*The project phase is a critical time to showcase your learning outcomes.*
