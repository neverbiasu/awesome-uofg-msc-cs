## 🚨 Urgent Reminders

- The **Week 3 deadline** is firm; missing it can affect your entire academic year.
- **Lab conflicts** are relatively easy to resolve; **lecture conflicts** require re-selecting courses.
- The **"choose 2 of 3" restriction** requires advance planning to avoid last-minute issues.

## 💡 Course Selection Strategy

1. **Plan Ahead**: Study the course timetables before the semester begins.
2. **Have a Backup Plan**: Prepare 2-3 alternative course combinations.
3. **Communicate Promptly**: Contact the Helpdesk immediately if you encounter problems.
4. **Be Flexible**: Lab times can often be adjusted, so take advantage of this.

## 📞 Contact Information

- **SoCS Helpdesk**: For general course selection questions.
- **BO1028 Consultation**: For face-to-face help with complex situations.
- **Course Coordinators**: For issues related to specific courses.
