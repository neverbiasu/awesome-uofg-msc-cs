## Overview

This week introduces the foundational concepts of cryptography, including course overview, mathematical background, polynomial arithmetic, and information theory basics. These concepts are essential for understanding modern cryptography.

- **Format**: Lectures
- **Topics**: Introduction to cryptography, modular arithmetic, polynomials, information entropy

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand basic cryptographic concepts and historical background
2. Master modular arithmetic and number theory fundamentals
3. Understand polynomial arithmetic applications in cryptography
4. Learn information theory and entropy concepts
