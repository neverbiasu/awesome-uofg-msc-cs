## Overview
This week explores the revolutionary shift from centralized banking to decentralized cryptocurrencies. We study the technical foundations of Bitcoin, including the blockchain data structure, Proof-of-Work consensus, and the inherent security and privacy trade-offs.
- Theme: Cryptocurrencies & Blockchain
- Key Topics: Bitcoin Protocol, Proof-of-Work, Merkle Trees, Double-Spending, and Pseudonymity.
