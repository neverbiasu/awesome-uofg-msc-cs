## Overview
This week explores the foundations of data integrity and source authentication. We study how message digests uniquely identify data, the importance of true randomness in cryptography, and how to distribute secrets among multiple parties using threshold schemes.
- Theme: Integrity, Randomness & Secret Management
- Key Topics: Hash Functions, Birthday Attack, PRNG vs. TRNG, Shamir's Secret Sharing.
