## Summary

This week explores data integrity and authenticity through Message Authentication Codes (MACs) and cryptographic hash functions. It also covers the generation of random numbers and threshold-based secret sharing schemes like Shamir's Secret Sharing.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Message Digests & MACs">
    Understanding the role of one-way hash functions in ensuring data integrity. Difference between a simple hash and a MAC (Message Authentication Code) which uses a secret key for authentication.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Birthday Attacks">
    Exploring collision resistance and the statistical probability of finding two messages with the same digest. Understanding the Birthday Paradox and its implications for digest size.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Random Numbers">
    Classification of random number generators: Pseudo-random (PRNG), Cryptographically Secure PRNG (CSPRNG), and True Random Numbers. Case study on historical flaws in Netscape's PRNG.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Secret Sharing">
    Techniques for splitting a secret among multiple parties. Introduction to Threshold Schemes (m, n) where any $m$ out of $n$ participants can reconstruct the secret, using Lagrange Interpolation.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
