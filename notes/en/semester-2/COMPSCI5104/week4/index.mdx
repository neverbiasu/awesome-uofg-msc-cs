## Summary
This week focuses on formal methods for identifying security threats and defining security requirements. It introduces the STRIDE model for categorization and Attack Trees for visualizing attack paths.
<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Security Requirements">
    Distinguishing between functional and security requirements. Understanding assets, vulnerabilities, and threats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="STRIDE Model">
    A framework for categorizing threats: **S**poofing, **T**ampering, **R**epudiation, **I**nformation Disclosure, **D**enial of Service, and **E**levation of Privilege.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Attack Trees">
    Using tree structures to represent the steps an attacker might take to reach a specific goal. Analyzing cost, technical ability, and probability.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
