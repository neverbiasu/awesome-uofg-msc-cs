## Overview
This week focuses on identifying and modeling potential system weaknesses. We transition from standard functional modeling (UML) to security-focused modeling using Misuse Cases to represent the actions of malicious actors and define mitigation strategies.
- Theme: Vulnerability Identification & Modeling
- Key Topics: Vulnerability Mapping, UML Review, Misuse Cases, Sequence Diagrams.
