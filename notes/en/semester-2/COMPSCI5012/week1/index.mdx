## Summary

This week establishes the structural foundations of web applications. It covers how systems are layered (System Architecture), how data is structured (Database Modelling), and how information is organized for user navigation (Information Architecture).

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="System Architecture">
    Evolution from **1-Tier** (Monolith) to **2-Tier** (Client-Server), **3-Tier** (Presentation, Logic, Data), and **N-Tier** architectures.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Database Modelling">
    The importance of data integrity. **Entity-Relationship Diagrams (ERD)** for conceptual design and **Relational Schemas** for logical design. One-to-One, One-to-Many, and Many-to-Many relationships.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Information Architecture (IA)">
    Structuring content for usability. Creating **Site Maps** to show hierarchy and **User Flows** to visualize the user's journey through the application.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
