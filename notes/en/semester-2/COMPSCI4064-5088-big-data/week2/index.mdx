## Overview

This week covers the fundamental challenges of distributed computing and introduces MapReduce as a solution. We also explore Hadoop, the most widely-used implementation of the MapReduce paradigm.

- **Format**: Video Lectures
- **Topics**: Compute challenges, MapReduce model, Hadoop framework

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand the challenges of distributed computing
2. Master the MapReduce programming model
3. Learn the architecture and components of Hadoop
4. Understand how MapReduce solves parallelization problems
