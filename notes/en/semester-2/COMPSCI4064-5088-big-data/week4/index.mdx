## Overview
- Topics: Apache Spark Architecture, Transformations, Actions

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand the core architectural components of Spark (Driver, Master, Worker, Executor).
2. Master the execution flow of a Spark job (DAG, Stage, Task).
3. Distinguish between Transformations and Actions in Spark.
4. Familiarize with common basic and advanced transformations (map, reduceByKey, etc.) and Actions.
