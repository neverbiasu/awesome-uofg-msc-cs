## Learning Objectives
1. Understand the motivation for using CNNs over traditional MLPs for image data.
2. Identify and explain the function of key layers in a CNN (Convolution, Pooling, Fully Connected).
3. Understand the concept of weight sharing and how it reduces the number of parameters.
4. Recognize common CNN architectures and their use cases.
