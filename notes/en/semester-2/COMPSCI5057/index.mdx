# COMPSCI 5057: Human Computer Interaction Design and Evaluation

This course introduces the principles and practices of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), focusing on user-centered design methodologies, usability evaluation techniques, and interface design principles.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Course Information">
    **Learning Objectives:**
    1. Understand fundamental HCI principles and theories
    2. Apply user-centered design methodologies
    3. Conduct usability evaluations and user studies
    4. Design effective and accessible user interfaces
    5. Analyze and improve existing interface designs
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Course Structure & Assessment">
    **Course Structure:**
    - Lectures: Theoretical foundations
    - Labs: Design exercises and prototyping
    - Projects: User research and interface design

    **Assessment:**
    - Coursework assignments
    - Design project
    - Final exam
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
