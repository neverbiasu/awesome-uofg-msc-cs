## Summary

This week focuses on the rigorous design of HCI experiments to evaluate user interfaces. It covers the definition of hypotheses, selection of variables (IV/DV), and choosing the appropriate experimental design (within vs. between subjects) to ensure validity and reliability.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Experimental Variables">
    Understanding **Independent Variables (IV)** (what you change), **Dependent Variables (DV)** (what you measure), and **Control Variables** (what you keep constant).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Experimental Designs">
    Comparing **Within-Subjects** (participants do all conditions) vs. **Between-Subjects** (participants do only one condition). Pros and cons regarding learning effects and individual differences.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hypothesis Testing">
    Formulating the **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$)** and **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$)**. Designing experiments to reject the null hypothesis with statistical significance.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
