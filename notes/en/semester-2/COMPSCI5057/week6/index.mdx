## Overview
This week explores how to capture high-level information about user experiences at scale using surveys. We also discuss how to combine surveys with qualitative methods (Mixed Methods) like Focus Groups to gain deeper insights into the "why" behind user data.
- Theme: Surveys & Mixed Methods
- Key Topics: Questionnaire Design, Survey Biases, Likert Scales, and Focus Groups.
