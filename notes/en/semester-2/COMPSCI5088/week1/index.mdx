## Summary

This week introduces the "Three Vs" of Big Data (Volume, Velocity, Variety) and explores the hardware and software paradigms required to process it. It contrasts vertical scaling (adding more power to one machine) with horizontal scaling (adding more machines), which is the foundation of modern distributed computing.
