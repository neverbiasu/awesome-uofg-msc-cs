## Summary

This week bridges the gap between Hadoop and Spark. It details the complex execution flow of Hadoop MapReduce, identifies its limitations (disk I/O, latency), and introduces Apache Spark as a memory-centric alternative featuring Resilient Distributed Datasets (RDDs) and Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs).

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Hadoop Execution Flow">
    A deep dive into how a MapReduce job runs: JobTracker, TaskTrackers, HDFS reads/writes, sorting, and shuffling. Understanding the heavy reliance on disk I/O.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hadoop Limitations">
    Why Hadoop is slow for iterative algorithms (like ML) and interactive queries. Issues with small files and high latency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Introduction to Spark">
    Spark's core value proposition: **In-Memory Computing**. Introduction to **RDDs** (immutable, partitioned collections) and **DAGs** (optimizing execution plans before running).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
